About us


Intro

I have not liked basketball all my life. One day,i bought and started reading slam dunk, slam dunk flipped a switch in me, and all of a sudden i liked playing basketball and watching basketball. That is why i decided to create this simulator, for everyone to enjoy, with the most iconic players from the NBA


How it works

The base of the simulation are player stats, which take on a fifa approach. Each player has a defensive stat and a attacking stat. The program genrates a random number between 80 and 200, deciding the amount of playes that the match has. If one team is attacking, the program randomly generates one of the 5 positions to get the ball. The attacking player then faces off against the defender, with the attacker keeping his attacking stat, and the defender losing 40 of his defending stat. it then generates a random number between 1 and the attack+defence stats of the players. if the number is below or equal to the attacking stat, the attacker gets the point, and else the deffender gets the ball and counterattacks with his team. 3 or 2 pointer depends on the position


After the amount of plays is zero, the game ends, and the score gets displayed. if you were logged inn, you get the win on the leaderboard, and the match gets added to your career, wher eyou can view your record. same happens if you lose


Why use NBA_simulator?

Personally, i think it is a lot of fun competing against your friends, which is the number one reason for why to use NBA_simulator. There is something special with winning against your friends, and being above them on the leaderboard.
